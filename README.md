# Wake Up Hartford

I am building a Progressive Web App (PWA) called Wake Up Wednesday for NAACP Greater Hartford. The app allows Hartford, CT residents to enter their home address and receive plain-language information about their housing conditions, neighborhood health, and life stability resources — all pulled from a connected Google Sheet.

The app is designed for residents who may not be tech-savvy. It must be mobile-first, easy to read, fast-loading, and emotionally reassuring in tone. It should feel like a trusted community guide, not a government form or a data dashboard.

The app has three main sections:

Your Building — housing code violations, evictions, utility shutoffs, 311 complaints

Your Neighborhood — census tract data, neighborhood report card

Your Life — resources for economic stability, health, and safety (coming soon)

Key facts:

Presented by NAACP Greater Hartford

Data partnership with CT Data Collaborative

Community event: Wake Up Wednesday · Every Wednesday · Unity Plaza · Barbour Street · Hartford

The app is a prototype now; real data will come from a live Google Sheet connected via API

This is not connected to real data yet — use realistic mock Hartford data for all demo screens
